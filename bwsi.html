BWSI EMBSEC TA Labs

Embedded Security Summer Program Teacher Assistant

Cambridge, MA

Massachusetts Institute of Technology

July 2024 - August 2024

  • Developed 2 qualitative Jupyter notebook labs for the Embedded Systems course, with an emphasis on Hardware Hacking, improving 20 students’ mastery in cryptographic techniques such as Diffie-Hellman and Hashing.
  • Enhanced student comprehension of authentication and encryption by writing a student reference guide for the WolfSSL Library, allowing students to efficiently navigate and apply the library in their hands-on firmware development project.
  • Assisted in testing and validating the students’ MITRE-CAR Ti-MCU board project, helping ensure successful programming, building, and flashing of embedded code while working with a newly introduced SSL Library.
