Project Description

Role：Hardware Lead

Davis, CA • Northwest AI Hub & Robert Mondavi Institute Winery • June 2026 - September 2026

  • Designing and documenting schematics that support the Software Lead’s Python program for the Dual-Axis Barrel Scanner, connecting the two motors, the Rotation Axis Motor and the Tilt Axis Motor, to the dedicated control PC and LCD display.
  • Assembling electrical connections powering the 24VDC components including the STEPPERMOTOR IDM57 and ESS17, soldering and securing wires, and crafting 3D-printed mounts using MCAD, while focusing on the safety of the Winemakers.
  • Conducting verification and validation, testing that the wiring and hardware for the Dual-Axis Barrel Scanner is safe, efficient, and capable of accurately scanning the wine barrels with the automated motors talking to the control PC through the RS485 cable.
